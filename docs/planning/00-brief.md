# 00 — Brief

> **First document in the planning chain.** Fill this before any technology is discussed. The point
> is to force the goal into words before the fun part (picking tools) hijacks the conversation.
> Half a page. If you can't answer these, you're not ready to choose a stack.
>
> - **Owner:** {name / role}
> - **Last updated:** {YYYY-MM-DD}

## What are we building?

{One or two sentences. Plain language, no tech. If a non-technical person can't follow it, rewrite.}

## Who is it for?

{The actual users. Be specific — "internal ops team of 5" is useful, "users" is not.}

## Why now?

{What problem exists today, and why it's worth solving now rather than never. What happens if we
don't build it?}

## What does success look like?

{The one thing this has to get right. Concrete and checkable where possible — "an order can be
placed and paid in under a minute", not "good UX".}

## What is explicitly out of scope?

{Just as important as scope. Name the things people will assume are included but aren't — for this
version.}

## Constraints that are already fixed

{Anything non-negotiable going in: a deadline, a required integration, a hosting constraint, a
regulation, an existing system it must talk to. These shape the stack decision, so surface them
here.}
